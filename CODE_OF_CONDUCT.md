# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors should keep technical discussion respectful, specific and evidence-based. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.

Disagreement about implementation, undocumented APIs, hardware safety or project scope is expected. Critique the change, evidence or design rather than the person.

## Scope

This applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests and other project-maintained spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its contributors. For conduct or security concerns that should not be public, use GitHub's private reporting facilities when available.
